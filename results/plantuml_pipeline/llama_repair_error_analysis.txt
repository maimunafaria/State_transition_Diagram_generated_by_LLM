Llama Repair Error Analysis
===========================

Evaluation scope
----------------
Model: Llama 3.1 8B Instruct
Raw generation method: Few-shot
Cases: 27
Validity definition: Official PlantUML syntax passes and no structural
validation errors or warnings remain.

Plain-language interpretation
-----------------------------

Syntax-Grounded Repair improved Llama's overall strict validity by two cases,
increasing the result from 15 of 27 to 17 of 27. It was somewhat better at
repairing unreachable states, orphan states, and choice nodes that lacked
guarded outgoing transitions.

Baseline Repair was better at repairing missing final transitions and
PlantUML syntax errors. Baseline solved all 12 initial missing-final errors,
whereas Syntax-Grounded Repair solved 10. Baseline solved one of the two
initial PlantUML syntax errors, whereas Syntax-Grounded Repair solved neither.

Syntax-Grounded Repair also introduced new PlantUML syntax errors in cases 32
and 80. Consequently, four Syntax-Grounded outputs remained syntactically
invalid: cases 16, 32, 78, and 80.

The unreachable-state problem remained difficult. Baseline solved 6 of 10
initial unreachable-state cases, while Syntax-Grounded Repair solved 7 of 10.
However, each method also introduced an unreachable-state problem in another
case.

Conclusion
----------

The most persistent Llama repair problems were:

1. Choice nodes without outgoing transitions.
2. Unreachable states.
3. Multiple initial-state transitions.
4. PlantUML syntax errors.
5. The missing initial transition in case 70.

Syntax-Grounded Repair achieved the higher overall validity percentage, but
it was not consistently better for every violation type. In particular, its
PlantUML syntax repair performance was worse than Baseline Repair.
