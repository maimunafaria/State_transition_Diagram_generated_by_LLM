Staff Member Class — Polished Requirement Specification

Functional Requirements
1. The system shall allow assigning a staff member to a client or adding them to a campaign.
2. The system shall allow removing assignments from staff members, returning them to an unassigned state.
3. The system shall enable staff members to switch between different assignments during their work period.
4. The system shall mark a staff member as unemployed when they leave the job.
5. The system shall archive the record of a former employee after some time has passed.

### Analysis:

**States:**
- Unassigned
- Assigned to Client
- Assigned to Campaign
- Unemployed
- Archived

**Initial State:**
- The system starts when a staff member is hired or created. Initially, the state is **Unassigned**.

**Final States:**
- **Unemployed**: When a staff member leaves their job.
- **Archived**: After some time has passed since they left.

**Events or Conditions:**
1. Assign to Client
2. Assign to Campaign
3. Remove Assignment
4. Switch Assignment
5. Leave Job
6. Archive Record

**Transitions:**
1. From **Unassigned**, upon "Assign to Client" → **Assigned to Client**.
2. From **Unassigned**, upon "Assign to Campaign" → **Assigned to Campaign**.
3. From any **Assigned** state, upon "Remove Assignment" → **Unassigned**.
4. Within **Assigned** states, upon "Switch Assignment" → transition between **Assigned to Client** and **Assigned to Campaign**.
5. From any active state (**Unassigned**, **Assigned to Client**, or **Assigned to Campaign**) upon "Leave Job" → **Unemployed**.
6. From **Unemployed**, after some time passes → **Archived**.

**Missing or Ambiguous Details:**
- The exact duration before a staff member is archived from the **Unemployed** state is not specified.
- The conditions under which a staff member can switch assignments are unclear (e.g., who initiates it, any constraints).
