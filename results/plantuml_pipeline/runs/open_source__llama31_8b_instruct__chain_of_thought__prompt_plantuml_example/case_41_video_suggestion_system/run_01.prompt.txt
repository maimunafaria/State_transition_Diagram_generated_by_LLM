Requirement:
Video Suggestion System — Polished Requirement Specification

Functional Requirements
1. The system shall receive a video suggestion request from a user.
2. The system shall identify the user who made the video suggestion request.
3. The system shall gather different types of videos simultaneously based on the user's profile or preferences, trending videos from the user’s area, videos with similar tags, videos from similar genres, and promoted videos .
4. The system shall add additional details such as thumbnails and view counts to the suggested videos.
5. The system shall send the suggested videos to the user.

=== Chain-of-thought analysis response ===
Here's the analysis of the requirement for a state machine model:

**States:**

* Idle (initial state)
* RequestReceived
* UserIdentified
* VideosGathered
* DetailsAdded
* SuggestedVideosSent

**Initial State:**
Idle - The system is in an idle state until it receives a video suggestion request from a user.

**Final States:**
SuggestedVideosSent - This could be considered the final state, as once the suggested videos are sent to the user, there may not be any further processing required.

**Events or Conditions:**

* Receipt of a video suggestion request (triggers transition from Idle to RequestReceived)
* Successful identification of the user who made the request (triggers transition from UserIdentified)
* Completion of gathering different types of videos (triggers transition from VideosGathered)
* Addition of additional details such as thumbnails and view counts (triggers transition from DetailsAdded)

**Transitions:**

1. Idle → RequestReceived (receipt of video suggestion request)
2. RequestReceived → UserIdentified (successful identification of user)
3. UserIdentified → VideosGathered (completion of gathering different types of videos)
4. VideosGathered → DetailsAdded (addition of additional details such as thumbnails and view counts)
5. DetailsAdded → SuggestedVideosSent (sending suggested videos to the user)

**Missing or Ambiguous Details:**

* What happens if there is an error in identifying the user or gathering videos?
* How does the system handle multiple requests from the same user simultaneously?
