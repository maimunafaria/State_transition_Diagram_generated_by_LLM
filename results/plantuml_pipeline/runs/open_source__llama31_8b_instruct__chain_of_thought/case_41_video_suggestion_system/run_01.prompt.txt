Video Suggestion System — Polished Requirement Specification

Functional Requirements
1. The system shall receive a video suggestion request from a user.
2. The system shall identify the user who made the video suggestion request.
3. The system shall gather different types of videos simultaneously based on the user's profile or preferences, trending videos from the user’s area, videos with similar tags, videos from similar genres, and promoted videos .
4. The system shall add additional details such as thumbnails and view counts to the suggested videos.
5. The system shall send the suggested videos to the user.

=== Chain-of-thought analysis response ===
Here is the analysis of the requirement for a state transition diagram:

**States:**

* Idle (initial state)
* RequestReceived
* VideosGathered
* DetailsAdded
* SuggestedVideosSent

**Initial State:**
Idle - The system starts in an idle state, waiting for a video suggestion request from a user.

**Final States:**
SuggestedVideosSent - The final state is reached when the suggested videos are sent to the user.

**Events or conditions:**

* Video suggestion request received from a user (triggers transition from Idle to RequestReceived)
* User identified (triggers transition from RequestReceived to VideosGathered)
* Different types of videos gathered (triggers transition from VideosGathered to DetailsAdded)
* Additional details added to suggested videos (triggers transition from DetailsAdded to SuggestedVideosSent)

**Transitions:**

1. Idle → RequestReceived (video suggestion request received)
2. RequestReceived → VideosGathered (user identified and different types of videos gathered)
3. VideosGathered → DetailsAdded (additional details added to suggested videos)
4. DetailsAdded → SuggestedVideosSent (suggested videos sent to user)

**Missing or ambiguous details:**

* How does the system identify the user who made the video suggestion request? (e.g., authentication, login required?)
* What happens if there are no suitable videos gathered for a user's request?
* Are there any specific conditions under which the system sends promoted videos?
