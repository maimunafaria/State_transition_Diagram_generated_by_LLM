Requirement:
Staff Member Class — Polished Requirement Specification

Functional Requirements
1. The system shall allow assigning a staff member to a client or adding them to a campaign.
2. The system shall allow removing assignments from staff members, returning them to an unassigned state.
3. The system shall enable staff members to switch between different assignments during their work period.
4. The system shall mark a staff member as unemployed when they leave the job.
5. The system shall archive the record of a former employee after some time has passed.

=== Chain-of-thought analysis response ===
**States:**

* Unassigned
* Assigned (to a client or campaign)
* Unemployed
* Archived

**Initial state:**
Unassigned

**Final states:**
Archived

**Events or conditions:**

* Assignment to a client or campaign
* Removal from assignment
* Staff member leaves the job (becomes unemployed)
* Time passes (archive record)

**Transitions:**

* Unassigned → Assigned (on assignment to a client or campaign)
* Assigned → Unassigned (on removal from assignment)
* Assigned → Unemployed (on staff member leaving the job)
* Unemployed → Archived (after some time has passed)
* Any state → Archived (after some time has passed)

**Missing or ambiguous details:**

* What is considered "some time" for archiving a record?
* Can a staff member be reassigned to a different client or campaign while already assigned to one?
