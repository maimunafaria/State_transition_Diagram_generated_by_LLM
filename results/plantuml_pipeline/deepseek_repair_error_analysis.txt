Plain-language interpretation
-----------------------------

DeepSeek repaired most missing initial transitions, missing final transitions,
duplicate transitions, choice-node guard problems, and orphan-state problems.

PlantUML syntax errors remained difficult. Three initial cases contained an
official PlantUML syntax error. Both repair methods corrected one of those
cases (case 32), but neither method corrected the syntax errors in cases 65
and 67. Therefore, PlantUML syntax repair was only partially successful:
1 of 3 syntax-error cases was solved, while 2 of 3 remained invalid.

The unreachable-state problem was not solved. Case 41 contained unreachable
states before repair, and the same states remained unreachable after both
Baseline Repair and Syntax-Grounded Repair. Thus, the success rate for the
observed unreachable-state error was 0 of 1.

Conclusion
----------

For DeepSeek, the two most persistent repair problems were:

1. PlantUML syntax errors: only 1 of 3 cases was solved.
2. Unreachable states: 0 of 1 cases was solved.

It is accurate to state that DeepSeek was unable to solve the remaining
PlantUML syntax errors and the observed unreachable-state error. However, it
should not be stated that DeepSeek solved no PlantUML syntax errors, because
case 32 was repaired successfully.
