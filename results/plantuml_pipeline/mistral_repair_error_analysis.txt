Mistral Repair Error Analysis
=============================

Evaluation scope
----------------

Raw generation method: RAG
Cases: 27
Validity definition: Official PlantUML syntax passes and no structural
validation errors or warnings remain.

Both repair methods used the same 27 frozen original RAG diagrams.

Plain-language interpretation
-----------------------------

Syntax-Grounded Repair improved Mistral's strict validity by two cases,
increasing the result from 8 of 27 to 10 of 27.

Its strongest improvement was orphan-state repair. Baseline repaired only
2 of 8 orphan-state cases, whereas Syntax-Grounded Repair repaired all 8.
Syntax-Grounded Repair also improved PlantUML syntax repair from 0 of 7 to
2 of 7, missing-final repair from 3 of 11 to 5 of 11, and unreachable-state
repair from 0 of 3 to 1 of 3.

However, five of the seven original PlantUML syntax-error cases remained
syntactically invalid after Syntax-Grounded Repair, and a new syntax error
was introduced in case 33.

Choice nodes without outgoing transitions remained difficult.
Baseline repaired 3 of 4 such cases, while Syntax-Grounded Repair repaired
only 1 of 4. Multiple initial-state transitions also remained common:
Baseline repaired 2 of 10, and Syntax-Grounded Repair repaired 3 of 10.

Conclusion
----------

The most persistent Mistral repair problems were:

1. PlantUML syntax errors.
2. Missing final-state transitions.
3. Multiple initial-state transitions.
4. Choice nodes without outgoing transitions.
5. Unreachable states.

Syntax-Grounded Repair was better overall, especially for orphan states.
Nevertheless, neither repair method was consistently effective across all
violation types.
