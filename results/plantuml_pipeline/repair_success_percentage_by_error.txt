Repair Success Percentage by Error Type
=======================================

Scope
-----

Models: Qwen 2.5 7B Instruct, Mistral, DeepSeek R1 14B, and
Llama 3.1 8B Instruct

Total model-case combinations: 108
Total initial violation-case occurrences: 161

A violation is counted once per case, even if multiple states or transitions
in that case exhibit the same violation.

Summary
-------

Syntax-Grounded Repair achieved the higher overall error-resolution rate:
63.98%, compared with 53.42% for Baseline Repair.

Syntax-Grounded Repair performed better for:

- Missing initial transitions
- Multiple initial transitions
- Unreachable states
- Orphan states
- PlantUML syntax errors
- Choice nodes without guards
- Invalid [*] to [*] transitions

Baseline Repair performed better for:

- Missing final transitions
- Duplicate transitions
- Choice nodes without outgoing paths

Although Syntax-Grounded Repair had a higher PlantUML syntax-error repair
rate, both methods performed poorly for that violation: 25.00% versus 16.67%.
