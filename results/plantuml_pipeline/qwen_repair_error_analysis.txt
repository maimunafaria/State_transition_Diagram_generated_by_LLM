----------------

Validity definition: Official PlantUML syntax passes and no structural
validation errors or warnings remain.

Important comparison note
-------------------------

The Baseline and Syntax-Grounded run folders do not contain identical initial
outputs for every case. Therefore, each repair method is evaluated against
the initial diagram stored in its own run folder. The overall final validity
percentages remain directly reportable, but per-error initial counts differ
slightly between the two runs.

Plain-language interpretation
-----------------------------

Syntax-Grounded Repair improved Qwen's strict validity by three cases,
increasing the result from 11 of 27 to 14 of 27.

The largest Qwen problem was multiple top-level initial-state transitions.
Baseline repaired only 2 of its 13 observed cases. Syntax-Grounded Repair
performed better, repairing 6 of its 14 observed cases, but 8 cases still
retained this violation.

Unreachable states were also difficult. Baseline repaired 2 of 5 observed
unreachable-state cases. Syntax-Grounded Repair repaired none of its 4
observed unreachable-state cases.

Both methods repaired most missing-final-state violations. Baseline solved
9 of 10, while Syntax-Grounded Repair solved 10 of 12. Neither method solved
its observed choice-node-without-outgoing-transition problem.

Baseline Repair introduced official PlantUML syntax errors in two outputs,
cases 15 and 39. Syntax-Grounded Repair did not introduce official PlantUML
syntax errors in this Qwen run.

Conclusion
----------

The most persistent Qwen repair problems were:

1. Multiple initial-state transitions.
2. Unreachable states.
3. Duplicate transitions.
4. Choice nodes without outgoing transitions.
5. Invalid [*] to [*] transitions.

Syntax-Grounded Repair was more effective overall for Qwen, particularly for
multiple initial-state transitions and guarded-choice problems. However, it
did not improve the observed unreachable-state errors.
