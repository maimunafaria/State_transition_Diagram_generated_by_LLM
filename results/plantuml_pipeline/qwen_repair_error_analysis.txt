=====================================

Evaluation scope
----------------
Model: Qwen 2.5 7B Instruct
Raw generation method: Original RAG
Cases: 27
Validity definition: Official PlantUML syntax passes and no structural
validation errors or warnings remain.

Controlled comparison
---------------------

Plain-language interpretation
-----------------------------

Syntax-Grounded Repair improved Qwen's strict validity from 11 of 27 to
14 of 27, an improvement of 11.11 percentage points over Baseline Repair.

The largest Qwen problem was multiple top-level initial-state transitions.
Baseline solved only 2 of 13 cases, while Syntax-Grounded Repair solved
6 of 13. Seven cases still retained this violation after Syntax-Grounded
Repair.

Syntax-Grounded Repair was also better for unreachable states, orphan states,
invalid [*] to [*] transitions, and both choice-node violation types.
However, Baseline Repair solved more duplicate-transition cases.

Both repair methods solved 9 of the 10 original missing-final-state
violations. Syntax-Grounded Repair additionally introduced a new missing
final transition in case 39.

Neither initial dataset contained an official PlantUML syntax error.
Baseline Repair introduced syntax errors in two cases, while Syntax-Grounded
Repair introduced syntax errors in three cases. Therefore, syntax corruption
during repair remains an important weakness.

Conclusion
----------

The most persistent Qwen repair problems were:

1. Multiple initial-state transitions.
2. Newly introduced PlantUML syntax errors.
3. Duplicate transitions.
4. Unreachable states.
5. Missing final-state transitions.

Syntax-Grounded Repair was more effective overall and repaired a wider range
of structural violations. Its main weakness was introducing three official
PlantUML syntax errors in otherwise structurally improved outputs.
